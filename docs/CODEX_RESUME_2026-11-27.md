## User-confirmed evaluation policy (2026-11-22)
- The AI should optimize for the **current actual board after special events have happened**, NOT attempt to anticipate the chance of any future 1% random events. Known active special rules still require correct play.
- Do NOT equate a reference engine move or fixed label mismatch with a blunder. The evaluator's `goodMoves` list can contain multiple equally acceptable moves, but archived attack problems label only the *shortest* forced wins within a specified horizon. For verified defense cases, a move not in the exhaustive safe list is proven to allow a short forced loss **only within the solver's specified rules and horizon**. For attack cases, non-membership in the shortest-win list is NOT proof the move is bad.

## User-confirmed strength objective and mate-distance tolerance (2026-11-22)
- **Primary goal:** retain as much of rsshogi-nnue-mini's ordinary-board playing strength as possible after a special event has changed the current position; do not optimize to imitate the independent referee's preferred moves.
